Please open AlbumEasy on a computer

The album builder needs a wide screen to lay out an A4 page and its stamp frames, so it is not available on phones and small tablets. Open this page on a laptop or desktop browser to paste your album script, add your images and download the PDF.

Supported AlbumEasy commands

Line based: one command per line. Text after # is a comment. Arguments go in brackets, text arguments in double quotes.

ALBUM_PAGES_SIZE (w h) Page size in mm (A4 is 210 297)
ALBUM_PAGES_MARGINS (l r t b) left, right, top and bottom margins in mm
ALBUM_PAGES_BORDER (o i x g) outer and inner line width in mm, gap before the inner line, and 1 for a border
ALBUM_PAGES_SPACING (h v) gap between items across and down a page, in mm
ALBUM_PAGES_FOOTER (id sz C "text") centred footer on every page
ALBUM_DEFINE_FONT (id "name") map a font id to a family, e.g. (RWB "Rockwell Bold")
ALBUM_STAMP_IMG_GRAYSCALE_ON/OFF greyscale the stamp images
PAGE_START begin a new album page
PAGE_TEXT_CENTRE (id sz "text") centred text; \n starts a new line, blank lines add space. LEFT and RIGHT also work
ROW_START / ROW_START_ES (…) begin a row of stamps, centred across the page
STAMP_ADD_IMG (w h "file" "date" "" "price") a stamp frame of w by h mm holding the image, with the date and price below it
PAGE_BACKGROUND_IMG ("file") stretch an image over the whole page

Fonts are matched to the closest of Times, Helvetica and Courier, so a Rockwell title prints as a bold serif. Image file names in the script must match the images you add.
