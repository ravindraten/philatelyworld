What is a "Phygital" Stamp?
Every stamp in this set is a physical piece of postage equipped with an NFC chip and a digital twin (NFT) on the Ethereum/Polygon blockchain. The RN4002 lot offers a rare opportunity to own the complete set of all six divine figures in their physical form, with their digital potential waiting to be unlocked.

The Six Pillars of Olympus

This collection is defined by six legendary figures, each representing the unique characteristics and landscapes of their issuing country:

Zeus (Austria)

The Father of the Gods and ruler of Olympus. He symbolizes leadership and innovation, reflecting Austria's role in founding this groundbreaking crypto series.

Hera (Austria)

The Queen of the Gods, embodying unity and loyalty. She pays homage to the enduring traditions and international partnerships within the philatelic family.

Hermes (Netherlands)

The messenger of the gods and patron of trade. His presence links the collection to the Netherlands' rich maritime and global commerce history.

Artemis (Luxembourg)

Goddess of the hunt and nature. She represents Luxembourg's determination and the wild, natural beauty of its diverse landscapes.

Athena (Belgium)

Goddess of wisdom and strategy. Her stamp stands for the intelligence and strategic role Belgium plays at the heart of the European Union.

Poseidon (Portugal)

The God of the Sea. He personifies the boundless energy of the water and the courage required to conquer the great unknown depths.

The Rarity Spectrum: The 5-Color Rainbow

While the physical stamps are stunning works of art, the true thrill for the collector lies in the digital "twin." Every stamp revealed via the QR code belongs to one of five rarity tiers: Black, Green, Blue, Yellow, or the ultra-scarce Red. Because the digital color is hidden behind a security panel on the physical stamp, the RN4002 set remains a "sealed mystery," preserving the excitement of the hunt for the elusive Red God.

Technological Security

The RN4002 set utilizes state-of-the-art NFC (Near Field Communication) technology. By simply holding an NFC-enabled smartphone to the stamp, collectors can verify its authenticity and ensure the digital twin has not yet been claimed. This guarantees that your investment is secure and that the blockchain-linked assets are genuinely yours.
