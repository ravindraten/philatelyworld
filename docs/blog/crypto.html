Divine Power on the Blockchain: The "Heroes of Mythology" Collection

Philately meets ancient legend in the groundbreaking "Heroes of Mythology" (CS 5.3) collection. This series marks a historic five-nation collaboration between Austria, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, Belgium, and Portugal.

What is a "Phygital" Stamp?
Every stamp in this set is a physical piece of postage equipped with an NFC chip and a digital twin (NFT) on the Ethereum/Polygon blockchain.

The Six Pillars of Olympus

Zeus (Austria)

The Father of the Gods and ruler of Olympus.

Hera (Austria)

The Queen of the Gods, embodying unity and loyalty.

Hermes (Netherlands)

The messenger of the gods and patron of trade.

Artemis (Luxembourg)

Goddess of the hunt and nature.

Athena (Belgium)

Goddess of wisdom and strategy.

Poseidon (Portugal)

The God of the Sea.

The Rarity Spectrum: The 5-Color Rainbow

Every stamp revealed via the QR code belongs to one of five rarity tiers: Black, Green, Blue, Yellow, or the ultra-scarce Red.
