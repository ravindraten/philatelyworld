The "SpongeBob und Freunde" Markenset: ten €0.95 self-adhesive stamps, first day of use 2 March 2026.

On 2 March 2026, Deutsche Post released a very special Markenset (stamp set): ten self-adhesive stamps to the value of €0.95 each — ideal for franking standard letters up to 20 g and postcards within Germany. But the striking feature of this issue goes far beyond the colourful design: every stamp is printed with interactive codes that play music when read with a pen from Ravensburger's tiptoi® range.

The Motif: An Icon of Pop Culture

When its creator Stephen Hillenburg (1961–2018) sketched his figure — a yellow, box-shaped sponge with bulging eyes and protruding front teeth — nobody could have predicted how iconic it would become. In 1999, SpongeBob SquarePants received its own animated series, which continues to this day and has produced numerous cinema films. The German premiere followed on 23 August 2002 on Super RTL.

SpongeBob lives in a pineapple in the underwater town of Bikini Bottom and is known for his bubbling cheerfulness and optimism, his naivety and his almost intrusive helpfulness. Thanks to the combination of child-friendly humour and clever allusions for adults, the series is regarded as a genuine classic of popular culture. One of its messages is the importance of friendship — celebrated on this special stamp, which shows SpongeBob together with his faithful friends Patrick and Sandy.

Printed with Interactive tiptoi® Codes

The stamps are printed with interactive codes. When read with the tiptoi® pen from Ravensburger, they bring out the music of SpongeBob's exciting world at Bikini Bottom — turning a quiet sheet of stamps into a mini sound toy. As with all Ravensburger tiptoi® products, the audio plays when the pen is pressed on the START button icon.

A stamp you can hear: touch the design with a tiptoi® pen and Bikini Bottom's music comes alive.

Getting the Audio onto Your tiptoi® Pen

To enjoy the interactive sound, you first need a tiptoi® pen (available from Ravensburger.de) and the corresponding audio file for this stamp. The setup is simple:

Buy a tiptoi® pen from Ravensburger.de.
Download the audio file for the SpongeBob stamp onto the pen — it will play the file when pressed on the START button icon, just as in all Ravensburger books.
Connect the pen to your PC via USB and copy the downloaded audio file (a ".gme*" file) into the drive of your tiptoi® pen.
Disconnect the pen, touch it to the stamp's START icon — and the music of SpongeBob's world plays.

A dedicated charger station can also transfer the audio file to the pen, but it is not necessary: the USB method works with any PC.

Why Collectors Care

The SpongeBob tiptoi® stamps are a delightful example of interactive philately — a product that is at once a pop-culture collectible (Stephen Hillenburg's iconic yellow sponge), a thematic treasure for cartoon and TV-music collectors, and a sound toy that children can actually play with using the tiptoi® pen. Whether you collect German special stamps, innovation stamps, or simply love SpongeBob, this Markenset is a fun and unusual addition to any collection.
