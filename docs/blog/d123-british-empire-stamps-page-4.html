Africa

Sheet 31

Gambia

350km strip along the Gambia River · English from 1618

Occupied by the English as early as 1618, Gambia gained internal self-government in 1965. Seven Victoria and Edward VII definitives are shown, plus three later George VI and QEII pictorials on the supplement page depicting palm-wine tapping and rice farming.

Sheet 32

Sierra Leone

"Lion Mountains" · settled by freed slaves

Named for its roaring surf, Sierra Leone became an English colony in 1788. Eight stamps span 1859 to modern issues, including a UPU 75th-anniversary commemorative and later pictorials of a lighthouse and the Orugu Bridge.

Sheet 33

Gold Coast (Ghana)

Independent 6 March 1957 — the first sub-Saharan colony to break free

Dutch, English and Danish traders settled the coast in the 17th century. This page runs Gold Coast War Tax overprints and a Silver Jubilee issue through to colourful independent-Ghana pictorials — a ginger flower, crowned cranes, a harbour scene and a hare.

Sheet 34

Togo

German protectorate to divided British/French rule, 1915

A German protectorate from 1884, Togo was split between British and French occupation from 1915. Stamps here include Dahomey/Togo overprints from the French occupation and a Benin telephone-centenary issue.

Sheet 35

Lagos & Southern Nigeria

British from 1861 · merged into Nigeria, 1914

Lagos was taken by the English in 1861, joined to the Southern Nigeria protectorate in 1906 and merged with Northern Nigeria into a single Nigeria on 1 January 1914. A long run of Lagos and Southern Nigeria Victoria and Edward VII definitives fills this sheet.

Sheet 36

Northern Nigeria

Protectorate stamps, 1900–1912

Northern Nigeria issued its own stamps from 1900 until its 1912–1914 merger with the south. Seven Edward VII portrait definitives are mounted on this supplement sheet, alongside a Togolese tree-planting stamp.

Sheet 37

Nigeria & the Niger Coast

Royal Niger Company territory to independent republic, 1960

The unified crown colony and protectorate of Nigeria won independence on 1 October 1960. A Kamerun colonial ship stamp and a Nigeria pictorial are shown here.

Sheet 38

British Somaliland

British protectorate zone from 1884 · merged into Somalia, 1960

England occupied the southern part of the Somali coast in 1884; the protectorate merged with Italian Somalia in 1960. Six stamps are shown, several on overprinted Indian-stamp bases.

Sheet 39

Seychelles

British from 1794 · independent republic, 1976

Named for a French naval officer, the Seychelles fell to England in 1794. Five stamps run from Great Britain and Mauritius-stamp usage through the islands' own issues, including a lighthouse pictorial and a fairy tern definitive.

Sheet 40

British East Africa (Kenya)

Chartered-company territory to independent Kenya, 1963

The Sultan of Zanzibar leased coastal territory to a British colonial company in 1887/89. Six stamps run from the Imperial British East Africa Company's first 1890 issue through modern Kenya Uhuru and warthog pictorials.
