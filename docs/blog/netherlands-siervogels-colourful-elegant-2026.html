Netherlands 2026: Siervogels, kleurrijk en elegant (Ornamental Birds – Colourful and Elegant)

Published: October 15, 2026

PostNL issued the Siervogels, kleurrijk en elegant (Ornamental Birds – Colourful and Elegant) stamp sheet on 14 October 2026. This special sheet of 10 stamps marks the annual Dag van de Postzegel (Day of the Stamp).

About the subject

Ornamental (fancy) birds are kept and bred for their beautiful plumage, shapes and colours. Most belong to the Galliformes (gamebirds), which spend much of their time on the ground and are not strong fliers – making them well suited to parks and gardens. The group also includes various waterbirds, pheasants, pigeons, cranes and finches. Fancy finches and pigeons are often kept in aviaries. The sheet features ten different ornamental bird species.

Design and designer

The stamp sheet was designed by Sandra Smulders (Vormgoed, Gouda). She selected ten of the most beautiful ornamental birds, carefully balancing colour, contrast and composition across the sheet while varying backgrounds and viewing angles. Some birds face each other; others look in different directions. The result is a lively whole that still allows each bird to stand out on its individual stamp.

To unify the design, Smulders added four gently curved horizontal lines that ripple across the sheet. Their thickness varies, and a transparent white gradient above/below the lines creates space and calm. Background colours echo those found in the bird photos, softly graduated from the corners to frame the sheet without distracting from the subjects.

Small details of each bird are shown in enlarged form above the top row and below the bottom row of stamps, drawing attention to their fine features. For the lettering, she chose Another Dreams by Typoraith Co – an elegant, slightly swirly typeface with two versions of capitals, which pairs well with the graphic lines.
