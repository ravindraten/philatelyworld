The new NL Crypto Stamp 5 – Demeter has been revealed! With Demeter, the Greek goddess of agriculture, fertility and nature, the latest edition introduces a central character who has captured the imagination for centuries.

Wheat ears, poppies and a commanding presence: every detail refers to the stories surrounding Demeter. The design brings together mythology, art and digital collectability in one truly distinctive issue.

Sales begin on 24 September at 10:00 am and will be available exclusively via NLCryptoStamp.nl. Are you ready to be among the first to discover Demeter?

Four Countries, Four Legends

This year, the Netherlands, Austria, Belgium and Portugal are once again joining forces for an international NL Crypto Stamp series. The theme, “Legends Meet Crypto Stamp – Horses and Heroes of Mythology”, brings together mythical heroes and gods, with a horse featured on every edition as a nod to the Year of the Horse 2026.

Netherlands

Demeter

Goddess of agriculture, fertility and nature — the striking new star of the series from PostNL.

Austria

Eos & Perseus

Austria chooses the goddess of the dawn and the legendary hero Perseus.

Belgium

Heracles

The mightiest of Greek heroes takes the spotlight in bpost's edition.

Portugal

Hades

Portugal looks to the enigmatic god of the underworld, Hades.

The five gods of the NL Crypto Stamp international series

Together with Demeter, they form a collection that transcends borders. The editions from the other participating countries will be available from 24 September via the websites of the participating postal organizations.

The international Crypto stamps are sold independently by each issuing post office, but together they form a single cross-border collection celebrating the myths and legends of Olympus.

Why Collectors Care

Demeter continues the story of digital philately in the Netherlands, blending a physical stamp with a digital twin (NFT) on the blockchain. For thematic collectors of mythology, art and modern Web3 collecting, the four-country set offers a compelling cross-border challenge — no crypto expertise required.
