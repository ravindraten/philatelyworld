Compilation "Innovation Exhibition Philately"

The Compilation "Innovation Exhibition Philately" by Austrian Post celebrates two decades of creative stamp development. This curated compilation includes eight original stamp masterpieces that represent remarkable innovation in design, material, and storytelling within modern philately.

Eight Iconic Stamp Masterpieces

The selection features distinctive issues including Enzian, Skispitze, Lederhose, Augarten Porcelain "Wiener Rose", a traditional beer coaster, the Venus of Willendorf, Corona, and Crypto Stamp 1.0. Together, these stamps highlight how Austrian philately blends cultural heritage with contemporary innovation.

With only 700 copies listed, this issue is particularly attractive for collectors who focus on limited annual compilations, thematic innovation, and flagship modern Austrian releases. It offers both visual appeal and strong long-term relevance in contemporary European philately.
