Gold Stamp: The Goldfinch (Het Puttertje)

PostNL presents a unique collector's item: The Goldfinch (Het Puttertje) by Carel Fabritius, immortalized on a stamp made of genuine 24-carat gold. This 1654 masterpiece is one of the absolute top pieces of the Mauritshuis in The Hague and is now available in a strictly limited edition for philatelists and art enthusiasts.

A Masterpiece in Detail

The Goldfinch is world-famous for its simplicity and the incredible technique with which Fabritius painted the bird's feathers and its shadow on the wall. In this gold edition, the texture of the original artwork is beautifully highlighted. The stamp is not only a tribute to Dutch painting but also a valuable precious metal object.

This special issue is delivered in a luxury collector's box, including a certificate of authenticity. It is part of PostNL's prestigious series of gold stamps, which focuses on the most important icons of Dutch cultural history.

Technical Specifications

Material 24-carat Gold (999/1000)
Subject The Goldfinch (Carel Fabritius)
Printing Technique Relief Embossing
Packaging Luxury cassette including certificate
Face Value 1 (Registered Mail Stamp)

Limited Availability

As is customary with PostNL's gold issues, the mintage is strictly limited(1000 copies only). This makes the item not only a beautiful addition to any stamp collection but also a sought-after piece for collectors of art-related memorabilia. Place order directly through philately world.
