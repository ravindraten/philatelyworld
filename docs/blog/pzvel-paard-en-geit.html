The Jaar van het Paard / de Geit frame stamp sheet, issued by PostNL on 10 November 2026

On 10 November 2026, PostNL issued the Jaar van het Paard / de Geit (Year of the Horse / Year of the Goat) kaderpostzegelvel. The sheet is a farewell to the Year of the Horse and a welcome to the Year of the Goat: each of the stamps shows one of the two animals, together symbolising the transition from one zodiac year to the next.

Two Zodiac Years on One Sheet

According to the Chinese calendar, the Year of the Horse gives way to the Year of the Goat on 6 February 2027. This stamp sheet celebrates that handover, and because the two animals sit next to each other in the zodiac cycle, the issue captures the exact boundary between two consecutive years.

The Chinese zodiac assigns an animal to each year in a repeating twelve-year cycle, and each animal is associated with its own character traits and traditions. The change of year is celebrated at Chinese New Year, which falls on a different date each year because the calendar follows the lunar cycle. The transition from the horse to the goat is therefore both an ending and a beginning — an idea the stamp sheet captures in its paired designs.

The horse is the seventh animal and the goat the eighth in the twelve-year Chinese zodiac cycle. The Year of the Horse ends and the Year of the Goat begins on 6 February 2027.

A Collectible Frame Stamp Sheet

The issue is a kaderpostzegelvel — a sheet of gummed frame stamps presented within an illustrated border — and forms part of PostNL's Chinese dierenriem (Chinese zodiac) series, which sits within the Kunst en cultuur theme. The sheet is interesting both for collectors who follow the Chinese zodiac and for collectors of PostNL's distinctive frame stamps.

PostNL has a long tradition of marking the Chinese New Year with specially designed stamps, and the horse-to-goat sheet continues that tradition with a design that looks both backwards and forwards. Because the issue belongs to a recurring series, it also works well as part of a larger thematic collection built around the Chinese calendar, animals, or the art of the frame stamp.

Why This Issue Matters

The Jaar van het Paard / de Geit sheet is a small celebration of a big idea: the turning of one year into the next. For collectors of Chinese zodiac stamps, it fills the gap between two annual issues with a single, elegant design. For everyone else, it is a colourful and culturally rich addition to a collection — and a reminder that the Chinese calendar continues to inspire some of PostNL's most distinctive stamp art.
