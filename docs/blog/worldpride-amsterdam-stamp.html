WorldPride Amsterdam 2026 (Stamp Sheet)

On 1 April 2026, PostNL released the WorldPride Amsterdam 2026 stamp sheet to mark a major global event that promotes visibility and awareness of LGBTQIA+ issues. The release date was chosen with special meaning: it commemorates 25 years since the first same-sex marriage was officially performed in the Netherlands.

Color, Symbolism, and Design

Graphic designer Martin Cadwallader created a vibrant, kaleidoscopic composition using geometric forms. The visual language is bold and celebratory, reflecting the spirit of pride, diversity, and inclusion. This issue stands out as both a modern postal item and a collectible piece linked to contemporary social history.
