This album was built the old-fashioned way: typed German catalog notes clipped and glued beside the stamps themselves, hand-painted maps marking out each territory, and the occasional postal-history cover tucked in for good measure. It follows the old geography of the British Empire — not modern borders, but the crown colonies, protectorates, condominiums and mandates as they actually issued stamps.

Africa

Sheet 51

Transvaal & Griqualand

Boer republic to Union province, 1910

Boer settlers founded the Transvaal free state from the late 1830s. Eighteen stamps span the South African Republic era through "V.R.I." and "E.R.I." war overprints to Edward VII portraits.

Sheet 52

Swaziland

Under Transvaal's shadow to independent kingdom, 1968

Placed under Transvaal's protection in 1894, Swaziland became a separate British protectorate in 1933. Eight stamps include a 1937 Coronation issue, Battle Axe and musical-instrument commemoratives.

Sheet 53

Orange Free State

Named for the Prince of Orange · Boer republic to Union province

Contested between England and France in the 1780s, the territory was settled by Boers from 1834. Ten "Oranje Vrij Staat" tree-emblem stamps run from 1868 issues to a "V.R.I." war overprint.

Sheet 54

South West Africa (Namibia)

Former German colony · League of Nations mandate to independence, 1990

Acquired by the Bremen merchant Lüderitz in 1883. Twelve stamps run from German colonial ship issues through South-West-Africa overprints to Fish River Canyon and quiver-tree pictorial series.

Sheet 55

Cape of Good Hope

Rounded by Dias 1488 · Dutch settlement from 1652

The Portuguese navigator Dias rounded the Cape in 1488. This page carries sixteen classic triangular and Hope-seated definitives from the 1850s to 1900s.

Sheet 56

Union & Republic of South Africa

Founded 31 May 1910 · republic from 1961

Formed on 31 May 1910 from the Cape, Natal, the Orange Free State and Transvaal. This page runs the first 1910 Union issues through modern RSA pictorials.

The Middle East & Arabia

Sheet 57

Oman, Kuwait & Bahrain

Gulf sheikhdoms under British postal administration

Muscat's independent sultanate ran a British-administered post office from 1856. Kuwait used overprinted Indian stamps before its own issues from 1958; Bahrain used unoverprinted Indian stamps until 1933; eight stamps run from India-design overprints through QEII portraits.

Sheet 58

Qatar

Overprinted British stamps from 1957

Qatar used overprinted Elizabeth II stamps of Great Britain from 1957 — the historical note is preserved here, though no stamps of Qatar itself survive mounted on this page.

Sheet 59

Palestine & Transjordan

British mandate 1918–1948 · Transjordan split off 1925

Britain ran a civil mandate from 1920 to April 1948. Six stamps include Egyptian Expeditionary Force overprints and an Israeli coin-design issue.

Sheet 60

Aden

Guarding the sea route to India · crown colony from 1937

Aden commanded the sea route to India; a British residency in southern Arabia from 1839, it became a crown colony in 1937. The sultanates of the Qu'aiti and Kathiri states issued their own stamps under Aden's protectorate.
