India Post Honors ISRO's Iconic Missions

In a historic tribute to India's burgeoning space capabilities, India Post has released a special set of commemorative stamps celebrating the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)
on April 12, 2026. These stamps mark significant milestones in the nation's journey toward the stars, coinciding with the inaugural National Space Day.

Celebrating the Space Odyssey

The new collection highlights four of ISRO's most ambitious projects. Primary among these is the Gaganyaan mission, India's first human spaceflight program. The stamps also immortalize the historic lunar success of Chandrayaan-3, the solar exploration mission Aditya-L1, and the XPoSat (X-ray Polarimeter Satellite) mission.

Technical & Issue Details

Name Commemorative Stamps: ISRO Milestones
Themes Included Gaganyaan, Chandrayaan-3, Aditya-L1, XPoSat
Issuing Authority Department of Posts, India
Occasion National Space Day 2024
Significance Human Spaceflight & Deep Space Exploration

Philatelic Importance

These stamps serve as a permanent record of India's scientific prowess and technological advancement. By documenting the transition from lunar landings to planned human spaceflight, the collection offers collectors a unique "space-chronology" in miniature. It is a must-have for those interested in scientific history and the evolution of the Indian space program.
