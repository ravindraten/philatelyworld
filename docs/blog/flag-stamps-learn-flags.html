Flag Stamps: Learn the Flags of the World

Long before a child becomes a stamp collector, they first learn to recognise the world around them - and few things are as colourful and memorable as a national flag. Flag Stamps is a free, browser-based game built for young learners aged 4 to 10. It turns the flags of nearly 200 countries into a playful, stamp-styled deck so kids can explore, listen and test themselves.

The learning idea is simple: tap a stamp to see a big, clear picture of the flag, read a friendly fun fact, hear the country name spoken aloud, and move through them continent by continent. When they are ready, a quick 10-question quiz turns that curiosity into a real challenge - with stars and a personal best score to beat.

Why Flags and Stamps Go Together

Flags are among the most popular themes in world philately. Flag stamps celebrate national days, independence anniversaries, the United Nations and joint issues between countries. Because every flag carries a story - a sun, a star, a dragon or a coat of arms - they make a perfect first collecting theme for a young enthusiast. Learning the flags early builds the geography and vocabulary that make browsing a stamp album far more rewarding.
