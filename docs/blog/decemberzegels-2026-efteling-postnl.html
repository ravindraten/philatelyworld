Decemberzegels 2026: Efteling Magic in a Snow Globe

Ten beloved Efteling animals drift into a winter wonderland on PostNL's festive 2026 stamp sheet

The Decemberzegels 2026 sheet — 20 stamps in 10 different designs, each with a winter snow globe motif

On 9 November 2026, PostNL releases the Decemberzegels 2026 stamp sheet, containing 20 December stamps in 10 different designs. This year the festive issue takes its inspiration from the Efteling, the famous Dutch fairytale theme park, and was designed by Efteling designer Jeroen Verheij.

Each stamp shows an animal from the park inside an elegant snow globe, each in its own little world. Together the ten designs form a warm, wintry tribute to the December season — and to the small moments of wonder that make it special.

The Design: Animals in Snow Globes

The December stamps of 2026 were designed by Jeroen Verheij, a designer at the Efteling. Ten different animals that visitors can encounter in and around the park sparkle inside ornate snow globes. Snow globes hold on to beautiful moments while everything around them carries on — and that is precisely what December is about: pausing for a moment to be together.

Look closely and each stamp reveals its own piece of the Efteling. Behind the animals appear silhouettes of well-known places and attractions. The peacock stands for Symbolica, the cat for Danse Macabre and the swan for Baron 1898. The Sprookjesbos (Fairytale Forest), Fata Morgana and other familiar spots also make an appearance.

Every animal received a winter detail of its own — a Christmas bauble, a candle, a sprig of holly or a little gift. There is even a wink to the postal world: the dove carries a letter away. The designs are soft, detailed and full of hidden discoveries for anyone who takes the time to look.

Meet the Designer: Jeroen Verheij

Jeroen Verheij has worked at the Efteling since 1997 and is a designer in the park's Design & Experience department. Over the years he designed souvenirs, brochures and interiors, and was responsible for the attraction Danse Macabre. The Decemberzegels of 2026 are the first postage stamps he has ever designed.

His background in immersive storytelling shows in the sheet: every globe feels like a tiny scene, complete with atmosphere, depth and a touch of theatre. The result is a festive issue that speaks to stamp collectors and Efteling fans alike.

"Betovering begint soms met iets kleins." — Enchantment sometimes begins with something small.

Using the Decemberzegels

A sheet contains 20 December stamps in 10 different designs. The stamps are valid from 9 November 2026 to 2 January 2027. After that period you can still use them for ordinary post — simply add extra postage up to the current rate for a regular stamp. Extra postage stamps (bijplakzegels) are available in the PostNL webshop or at a PostNL point.

  • Place 1 December stamp on a Christmas or New Year card up to 50 g within the Netherlands.
  • Your card should measure at least 14 × 9 cm and at most 32 × 22.9 cm, and be no more than 1 cm thick.
  • Is your card heavier than 50 g? Add extra postage up to the correct rate.
  • For a Christmas or New Year card up to 50 g to any destination worldwide, use 2 December stamps.
  • If you still have older December stamps with a printed value, add extra postage up to the current rate for a regular stamp.

Free Efteling Storybook

Collector Appeal

December stamps have long been one of the most popular seasonal issues in the Netherlands, and the 2026 edition brings a fresh, fairytale twist. The Efteling theme, the hidden attraction silhouettes and the first-ever stamp designs by Jeroen Verheij all make this sheet stand out.

The issue sits at the crossroads of several collecting themes: Christmas and winter philately, Dutch modern issues, theme park and fairytale collecting, and illustrated stamps. While stocks last, each sheet is a colourful, story-rich addition to any collection — and a charming way to send festive greetings.

Why It Matters

The Decemberzegels 2026 are more than a seasonal greeting in miniature. They celebrate connection — the act of pausing, writing and sending a card to someone who matters. With their Efteling charm, hidden details and warm winter palette, they capture the enchantment of December in ten small snow globes.
