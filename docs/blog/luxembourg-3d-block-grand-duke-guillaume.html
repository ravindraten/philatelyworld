The 3D souvenir sheet of HRH Grand Duke Guillaume — Luxembourg's first 3D-printed stamp.

On 22 June 2026, POST Philately celebrates a historic first for the Grand Duchy with a 3D block of HRH Grand Duke Guillaume — Luxembourg's first 3D-printed postage stamp. The unique philatelic souvenir sheet features a flexible, soft and pleasant-to-the-touch surface, marking a brand-new philatelic experience.

A New Philatelic Experience

Luxembourg's first 3D-printed stamp is a unique philatelic souvenir sheet with a flexible, soft and pleasant-to-the-touch surface. Because of the materials used in the production of the block, there will exceptionally be no special cancellation for this issue.

What is 3D Printing for Stamps?

A liquid photopolymer is applied onto the stamp sheets in extremely fine droplets using high-precision nozzles, then cured under UV light before subsequent droplets are applied. The monogram is thus built up layer by layer at different heights — giving the stamp its tactile, three-dimensional character.

Why Collectors Care

Hot on the heels of Austria's pioneering 3D-printed Edelweiss stamp, POST Luxembourg joins the exclusive club of postal administrations using additive 3D manufacturing — this time produced by the same firm, Hämmerle & Vogel. With a print run of just 1,000 blocks, the Grand Duke Guillaume souvenir sheet is an extremely limited, tactile landmark of 2026 philately for collectors of Luxembourg stamps, royalty / monarchy themes, and innovative stamp technology.
