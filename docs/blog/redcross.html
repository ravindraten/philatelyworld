Social Philately

The Red Cross: A Sixty-Year History of Philanthropic Postage

The Red Cross theme is a pillar of social philately. This expansive collection (RN4089) is a monumental archive housed in 8 professional albums, documenting the humanitarian efforts of the 20th century across various nations.

"This lot provides a rare opportunity to study the evolution of 'Semi-Postal' issues—stamps sold with a surcharge to fund medical and war relief efforts."

Rarities and Variety Study

Specialists will find deep value in the inclusion of Imperforate varieties and First Day Covers (FDCs). Covering the timeline from 1917 to 1980, the collection allows for a comparative study of wartime production versus post-war reconstruction issues.
