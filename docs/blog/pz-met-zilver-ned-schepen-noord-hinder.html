Postzegel met zilver – Nederlandse Schepen – Noord Hinder, the final issue in the 2026 silver ship series

In 2026, PostNL issued a new four-part series of stamps with silver devoted to Dutch ships built in the twentieth century. The four vessels are Zwarte Zee, Willem Ruys, Oranje Nassau and Noord Hinder. The final stamp in the series honours the Noord Hinder, the last Dutch lightship.

A Floating Lighthouse

The Noord Hinder was a 45-metre lightship with a fixed position west of Walcheren, near the Noord-Hinder sandbank. It served not only as a floating lighthouse but also carried a range of instruments for measuring water and air temperature, wave height, wind speed and cloud cover. Its daily observations were made for the Koninklijk Nederlands Meteorologisch Instituut (KNMI), the Royal Netherlands Meteorological Institute.

Lightships like the Noord Hinder were essential to safe navigation in waters where a permanent lighthouse could not be built. They marked dangerous shallows and shipping routes, and their crews lived on board for long periods, maintaining the light and recording the weather in some of the busiest and most exposed waters of the North Sea.

On 21 March 1994 the Noord Hinder was definitively taken out of service as the last Dutch lightship and brought to Hellevoetsluis. As a museum ship at the Koningskade, it is now open to the public.

The 2026 Dutch Ships Silver Series

The Noord Hinder is the fourth and final stamp in PostNL's 2026 series of Dutch ships with silver. The series brings together four very different vessels, each with its own place in Dutch maritime history: the tug Zwarte Zee, the ocean liner Willem Ruys, the Oranje Nassau and the lightship Noord Hinder.

The stamps are produced with silver and presented in a luxury passe-partout, reflecting the Collect Club's approach to special issues: a limited edition, a refined presentation, and a subject with a strong story behind it. For collectors of maritime themes, the series forms a coherent set that can be displayed together, with the Noord Hinder as its closing chapter.

Why This Issue Matters

The Noord Hinder silver stamp closes a series that looks at Dutch ships as technical achievements, as workplaces, and as symbols of the country's relationship with the sea. As the last Dutch lightship, the Noord Hinder carries a particularly strong story: a vessel that guided others safely home and now survives as a museum ship. For collectors of maritime history and silver stamps, it is a fitting finale to the 2026 Dutch Ships series.
