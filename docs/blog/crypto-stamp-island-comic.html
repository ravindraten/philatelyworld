The two issues of Crypto stamp Island

The first issue of "Crypto stamp Island" was developed in collaboration with the founders of ASH (Austrian Superheroes), bringing the world of Crypto stamps to life. "Return from Darkness" exists in a strictly limited edition of only 2,000 copies — each comic book has a unique numbering and many other surprises. Its sequel, "Battle of the Mirrors", continues the story with an unstable portal, a dark version of reality, and a key that could change everything. A third issue is already in development.

An NFT to protect Crypto stamp Island

Every issue of "Return from the Darkness" also includes an integrated NFT which is woven into the storyline, giving all comic owners an active role in the story. The NFTs follow the same colour scheme and distribution pattern as traditional Crypto stamps — red, yellow, green, blue, and black — so collectors instantly recognise the familiar rarity tiers.

One comic book, one numbered copy, one NFT woven into the story — the perfect bridge between classic collecting and Web3 ownership.

The Crypto stamp Island product series

The new product series builds on the great success of the CRYPTO STAMP ISLAND comic. Two issues have already been released, with a third currently in development. In addition, the popular characters have received their own Crypto stamps, and numerous other figures, locations, and artifacts have been immortalized on collectible cards. As a result, the world of Crypto stamp Island continues to grow, offering collectors an ever-expanding range of exciting highlights.

Additional stamps: Locations, Artifacts & Characters

Each Crypto stamp set includes one Crypto stamp with a face value of €11.35, as well as three randomly selected additional stamps with a face value of €2 each. Every set contains exactly one additional stamp from each of the following categories: Locations, Artifacts, and Characters.

The most sought-after items are the holo variants, which appear in only 1% of all sets. These rare cards add an exclusive holographic shine to any collection and make opening each pack an exciting moment.

Locations like the Alien Ship and the Bear Materialize, Artifacts like the Zaubertisch, Firestone and Prism Key, and Characters like Nox Nocta, Mr Blue and the Scale Monster — every pack brings a collectible journey across the island.

Why Collectors Care

Crypto stamp Island is one of the most charming bridges between classic philately and the Web3 world. A physical, individually numbered comic book carries an NFT into the story itself, while the Crypto stamps, trading cards, and the €11.35 + €2 additional-stamp pack format bring a playful, collectable rhythm to the whole series. For comic-lovers and crypto-philatelists alike, it is an accessible way to dive into phygital collecting — no crypto expertise required.
