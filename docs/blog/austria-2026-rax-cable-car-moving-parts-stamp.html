On 12 June 2026, Austrian Post issued a special stamp to commemorate the 100th anniversary of the operation of the Rax Cable Car. The Rax is one of Vienna's landmark mountains and an important recreational area close to the city. The Rax Cable Car was the first aerial cableway for passenger transport in Austria, making this anniversary an important milestone in Austrian engineering history.

The miniature sheet is shaped like a modern cable car cabin with doors that open.

Interactive Design with Moving Parts

What sets this issue apart is its interactive design. The stamp is placed inside a miniature sheet shaped like a modern cabin car, complete with doors that open outwards. When opened, the doors reveal the actual stamp featuring a historic photograph of the original Rax Cable Car. This clever use of moving parts transforms a simple stamp issue into a miniature model, engaging collectors through touch and interaction.

This type of "stamps with moving parts" issue is a collector favourite, as it brings the subject to life and creates a multi-sensory experience that goes beyond traditional philatelic design.

The Rax Cable Car holds a special place in Austrian history as the country's first aerial cableway designed for passenger transport — a fitting subject for such an innovative commemorative issue.
