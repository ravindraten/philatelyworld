Commemorating 70 Years of Diplomatic Ties: Lao PDR & India unilateral Postage Stamp Issue

The official ceremony marking the release of the Lao-India joint commemorative postage stamp.

In a significant gesture of friendship and cooperation, the Ministry of Technology and Communications of Lao PDR, in collaboration with the Indian Embassy in Vientiane, released a special joint postage stamp on **March 20, 2026**. This issue commemorates the **70th Anniversary of Diplomatic Ties** between the two nations (1956-2026).

The stamp symbolises the deep civilizational and cultural bonds that have connected Laos and India for centuries, rooted in shared heritage such as Buddhism and the legendary Ramayana (known locally as the Phalak Phalam or Ramakien).

Technical Specifications

Date of Issue March 20, 2026
Denomination 20,000 Kip
Quantity 3,000 Sets (Limited Edition)
Designer Vongsavanh DAMLONGSOUK
Printing Method Offset

The 20,000 Kip stamp beautifully incorporates motifs reflecting the shared history of both countries.

A Partnership Rooted in History and Development

India and Lao PDR share a long-standing partnership marked by extensive cooperation across various sectors. The unilateral stamp issue serves as a testament to the growth of these relations over the past seven decades.

Development and Infrastructure

India has extended Line of Credits (LoCs) exceeding **USD 236 million** to Lao PDR, supporting vital projects in agriculture (irrigation), electricity transmission, and infrastructure development. These initiatives have played a crucial role in enhancing the socio-economic landscape of the country.

Cultural Preservation: Vat Phou

One of the highlights of Lao-India cooperation is the restoration of **Vat Phou Champasak**, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Since 2006, Indian experts have been leading the conservation efforts, preserving this significant piece of Southeast Asian history and architecture for future generations.

Representatives from both nations celebrating the 70th anniversary through philately.

Human Resource & Education

India continues to support Lao PDR’s human resource development through the Indian Technical and Economic Cooperation (**ITEC**) programme. To date, over **1,500 Lao officials** have received training in various fields. Furthermore, India provides annual scholarships under the Mekong-Ganga Cooperation (MGC) and ICCR schemes, fostering academic exchange and expertise.

Did you know? During the COVID-19 pandemic, India supported Lao PDR with a gift of 50,000 Covovax doses in early 2023. More recently, in 2024, India provided a grant of USD 1 million for a Food Fortification Project to enhance the nutritional health of the Lao people.

This joint stamp issue is more than just a collector's item; it is a bridge of friendship that celebrates a shared past and a promising future of collaboration between Lao PDR and India.
