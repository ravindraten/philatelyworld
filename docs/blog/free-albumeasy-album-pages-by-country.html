Album pages are the part of collecting nobody enjoys making. Rows of boxes, a title, dates, a catalogue price, a place for the stamp — and every one of them has to line up perfectly on the page. The AlbumEasy project solved that problem years ago, and its community has quietly built up a library of 456 free album PDFs covering 116 countries. What is inside

The library leans heavily on the classic collecting countries: United States (47), Germany (34), Portugal (23), Canada (17), France (17) and more. But the long tail is the interesting part — there are single albums for places most catalogues reduce to a footnote: Aden, Basutoland, Fujeira, Ifni, Ruanda-Urundi, Zululand, the Cocos (Keeling) Islands. If you have a shoebox of commonwealth stamps with no home, there is very likely a page here waiting for them.

Alongside the country albums there is a section of topical and omnibus albums — themes that cut across borders, for collectors who follow a subject rather than a nation.

How to print these pages properly

  • Print at 100%. In the print dialog set scaling to Actual size or 100% — never Fit to page. Album pages are laid out to the millimetre, and scaling breaks the alignment of the printed boxes.
  • Use A4 paper, 100–120 gsm. Ordinary 80 gsm copier paper works but buckles once stamps are mounted on it.
  • Print single-sided unless the album is designed for duplex; thick paper plus a second side makes the sheet curl.
  • Mount, don't glue. Use stamp hinges for used stamps and acid-free mounts or stock cards for mint — PVC-free mounts will not damage the gum over time.
  • Check the layout first. Print page one of an album as a test sheet, lay your stamps on it, then print the rest.

Where these albums come from

Every PDF here is made and shared free of charge by individual collectors using AlbumEasy, the free album-designing software, and distributed through the project's public file archive. The name of the collector who created each album is printed with the entry, so you always know whose work you are printing. Nothing on this page is sold, paywalled or watermarked; the only ask of the original authors is that the pages are used for personal collecting.

A note on the download buttons: we are mirroring the full library to our own storage so that the files stay available. Until that mirror goes live the buttons are inactive — every album listed here is already published, and the links switch on album by album as the files land.
