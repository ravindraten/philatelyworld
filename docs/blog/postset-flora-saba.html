The Postset Flora Saba: an uncancelled stamp sheet with 3 stamps and 3 matching postcards

The Postset Flora Saba brings together two ways of collecting in one elegant package: a sheet of stamps and a set of postcards. Issued by PostNL as part of the Beleef de natuur (Experience Nature) series, the postset features an uncancelled stamp sheet with 3 stamps and 3 unique postcards, all illustrated by Medy Oberendorff.

Saba: A Caribbean Island of Plants

Saba is the smallest special municipality of the Netherlands, a volcanic island in the Caribbean with a landscape dominated by Mount Scenery and a coastline of steep cliffs. For Dutch standards its plant life is extraordinary: the island is home to a remarkable variety of special trees and plants, from the West Indian mahogany to the West Indian Tree Fern, an ancient, tree-like fern that can grow seven to ten metres tall.

That richness is the subject of the Beleef de natuur series, a multi-year project devoted to the Caribbean Netherlands. In 2026 the series focused on Saba, moving through the island's birds, butterflies and underwater life before arriving at its flora. The flora sheet celebrates the plants of the island, and the matching postset offers a more personal way to enjoy them.

The stamps of the Postset Flora Saba include the Caribbean bladderwort (Caribisch blaasjeskruid), a fascinating carnivorous plant that belongs to the island's unusually rich flora.

Stamps, Cards and a Single Design Language

A PostNL postset is more than a stamp sheet. It combines an uncancelled postzegelvel with three unique postcards in the same design. Each stamp has a matching card, so the set can be used to send a postcard with a perfectly coordinated stamp — or kept intact as a collectible. The format has become a favourite among collectors of modern Dutch stamps because it sits between a stamp issue and a small work of art.

The illustrations are by Medy Oberendorff, who also illustrated the Postset Vlinders Saba (Postset Butterflies Saba). Her work for the flora set captures the most striking plants of the island, translating Saba's dense, tropical vegetation into clear, elegant images that work equally well at stamp size and on a postcard.

Why This Issue Matters

The Postset Flora Saba is a reminder that the Netherlands is more than its European mainland. By pairing stamps with postcards, it invites collectors to look closely at the plants of Saba and to use the set as it was intended — to send something beautiful through the post. For collectors of the Beleef de natuur series, Caribbean themes, or the illustrations of Medy Oberendorff, it is a natural companion to the flora stamp sheet.
