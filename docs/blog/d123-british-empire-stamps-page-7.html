Stamps of the British Empire: A World Tour

This album was built the old-fashioned way: typed German catalog notes clipped and glued beside the stamps themselves, hand-painted maps marking out each territory, and the occasional postal-history cover tucked in for good measure. It follows the old geography of the British Empire — not modern borders, but the crown colonies, protectorates, condominiums and mandates as they actually issued stamps.

The Middle East & Arabia

Sheet 61

Iraq

League of Nations mandate 1921 · republic from 1958

A handwritten note records the British League of Nations mandate from 2 March 1921, the kingdom from 23 August 1921, and the republic from 14 July 1958; Turkish stamps with surcharges served the territory as early as 1918. A single Republic of Iraq commemorative — a stylised sun-and-flame design — is mounted here.

South Asia

Sheet 62

India

East India Company to Republic, 26 January 1950

Vasco da Gama discovered the sea route to India in 1498. A British India Universal Postal Union postcard from Bombay sits above a long run of Victoria, Edward VII and George V definitives, official "On H.M.S." overprints, and "SERVICE" officials.

Sheet 63

Ceylon (Sri Lanka) & the Maldives

Portuguese to Dutch to British · republic as Sri Lanka, 1972

The Portuguese settled the coast from 1517. This page runs twelve classic Victoria and George V definitives plus War Stamp overprints, with a further page of Silver Jubilee, New Constitution and Independence commemoratives.

Sheet 64

Burma

Treaty state of India to independent republic, 1948

A treaty state of India from 1826, Burma was fully absorbed into British India in 1886 and became an independent republic on 4 January 1948. Six stamps include Japanese-occupation overprints and Union of Burma pictorials.

Southeast Asia & the Far East

Sheet 65

North Borneo (Sabah)

North Borneo Company territory to Malaysian statehood, 1963

The Sultan of Sulu transferred his rights to the North Borneo Company in 1881. Three stamps run from an 1883 half-cent issue through a tapir pictorial to a protectorate three-cent design.

Sheet 66

Sarawak, Brunei & Labuan

Three small territories on Borneo's north coast

The Sultan of Brunei ceded Sarawak to Sir James Brooke in 1842. Its first stamps date to 1869; two Sultan-portrait stamps from Brunei and a monkey pictorial from Labuan are shown.

Sheet 67

Straits Settlements & the Federated Malay States

Crown colony founded 1867 · sultanates federated 1895

Seven stamps run Queen Victoria through George V, with a modern Singapore shellfish pair. Six Federated Malay States stamps here include a Johore issue.

Sheet 68

Cocos (Keeling) Islands

Australian administration since 1955

Five pictorial stamps show a white tern, coral formations, a fish and a tropicbird.

Sheet 69

Hong Kong & Shanghai

Crown colony from 1843 · plus a curious local post system

A British crown colony from 1843, Hong Kong issued its own stamps from 1862; fourteen stamps here run Victoria through modern QEII and Lunar-calendar issues.

Sheet 70

Papua & New Guinea

British protectorate, German colony, and Australian mandate, united 1949

British New Guinea became a protectorate in 1883/84. Stamps trace a German colonial ship issue through Papua & New Guinea pictorials of huts, war canoes and birds of paradise.
