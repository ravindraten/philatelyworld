Digital Philately: A Guide to Using bpost’s StampConnect App

The world of stamp collecting is evolving. Gone are the days when your collection was confined to heavy physical albums at home. With the launch of the StampConnect app—a collaboration between Bephila vzw and bpost—collectors can now carry their entire philatelic world in their pocket.

What is StampConnect?

StampConnect is a free mobile application dedicated to stamp enthusiasts. It serves as a bridge between the traditional hobby of philately and the digital age. It allows you to create virtual albums, scan your physical stamps, and interact with a community of like-minded collectors.

Key Features of the App

  • Virtual Albums: Organize your stamps into customized or official collections.
  • Instant Scanning: Use your smartphone camera to add stamps to your digital gallery.
  • Community Sharing: Share your progress with friends, children, or grandchildren.
  • Gamification: Earn badges and unlock levels as you complete your collections.
  • Expert Database: Search a vast database to identify and learn about specific stamps.

Step-by-Step Guide: How to Use StampConnect

1. Download and Register

The app is available for free on both the Google Play Store and Apple App Store. Once downloaded, you can create an account using your email address. If you already have a bpost account, you can often use the same credentials to streamline your experience.

2. Starting Your First Collection

On the main dashboard, you will see a green "+" sign at the top. Tap this to start a new collection. You have two options:

  • Own Collection: Create a blank canvas for your unique finds.
  • Existing Collection: Choose a shared collection (e.g., from Bephila) to see which stamps you are missing.

3. Digitizing Your Stamps

This is where the magic happens. To add a stamp:

  • Open the app and select the Camera icon.
  • Align your stamp within the frame and take a clear photo.
  • Alternatively, you can upload a high-quality scan from your phone's Gallery.

4. Connecting with Others

Stamp collecting is better together! Use the "Friends" icon to search for friends via their email addresses. You can invite family members to view your collection or collaborate on the same digital album.

Why Should You Use It?

The app provides portability, allowing you to show your collection at fairs without carrying heavy albums. It also acts as a preservation tool, creating a digital backup of your inventory for insurance or trading purposes.

Download StampConnect today and bring your favorite stamps back to life in the digital world!
