On 10 November 2026, PostNL issued the Oldtimer Vrachtwagens (Vintage Trucks) kaderpostzegelvel. The sheet contains five different stamps, each devoted to a classic Dutch truck, and was designed by Anne Schaufeli and Huub de Lang of Studio026.

What Is an Oldtimer Truck?

Oldtimer trucks are trucks that are forty years old or older. They are kept for many different reasons: car and transport companies preserve one or more as a showpiece or for promotional use at anniversaries, while museums such as the Metropole Museum Druten and the DAF Museum maintain collections of historic commercial vehicles or display scale models. Private owners keep them for nostalgic reasons — some collectors have more than forty.

The enthusiast community is large and well organised. Clubs such as the DAF Oldtimer Truck Club and the HAV – Vrachtwagengroep have hundreds of members and together own thousands of classic trucks, and they regularly organise joint tours and events. At the same time, these vehicles are part of a bigger story: the history of Dutch transport, the growth of the economy, and the development of the country's infrastructure.

Design and Designers

Studio026 — Anne Schaufeli and Huub de Lang, Velp

Studio026 is a graphic design studio in Velp made up of Anne Schaufeli (1987, Warnsveld) and Huub de Lang (1980, Arnhem). Both studied graphic design at ArtEZ Hogeschool voor de Kunsten in Arnhem between 2004 and 2008. They work conceptually, taking time for research, experiment and innovation, and design books, posters, magazines, catalogues, identities, infographics and other graphic work.

For PostNL they previously designed 't Oude Ambaght (2026), Historische trams (2025), Op safari in Nederland (2024), De magie van André Rieu (2024), 125 jaar KNLTB (2024), Leidens ontzet 450 jaar (2024), Prehistorische dieren (2023–2024), 10 jaar koning Willem Alexander (2023), Juliana der Nederlanden (2022), Paleis Het Loo (2022), Kijk op Nederland (2022), Historische motorfietsen (2021), Oude postroutes (2020) and 150 jaar Rode Kruis in Nederland (2017).

The designers describe how they wanted to bring together two very different aspects on one sheet: the community of enthusiasts who organise tours and events, and the great significance of vintage trucks to the transport history of the Netherlands — and therefore to the economy and the development of the infrastructure.

The sheet shows five truck makes: Kromhout, DAF, GINAF (Van Ginkels Automobielfabrieken), FTF (Floor Truck Fabriek) and HOGRA. In the designers' own description, the photographs were edited to refresh the colour and accentuate the lines, almost as if the trucks were miniatures. The vehicles do not always wear their original colours, because new owners often repaint them in a favourite shade — yet they all fit within a single scheme of bold, saturated colour.

Why This Issue Matters

The Oldtimer Vrachtwagens sheet is a tribute to the machines that helped build modern Dutch transport — and to the people who keep them running. Its combination of documentary photography and vivid colour gives the trucks a presence that works beautifully at stamp size. With an edition of 6,000 sheets, it is also a genuinely collectible issue for anyone interested in transport history, industrial design, or the graphic work of Studio026.
