The Nature of Belgium: André Buzin’s Avian Masterpieces

André Buzin is widely regarded as one of the most significant wildlife artists in the history of European postage. His work for the Belgian Post has transformed the utilitarian definitive stamp into a medium for high-fidelity nature illustration.

The Technical Art of the Bird Series

Since the series began in 1980, Buzin has focused on "Biological Realism." Every issue in this lot (RN4103) demonstrates his signature attention to plumage detail and the specific lighting of the Belgian countryside.

Item RN4103 Details:
  • Condition: Mint Never Hinged (MNH)
  • Theme: Ornithology / Fauna
  • Country: Belgium
  • Design: Original artwork by André Buzin
