#NFTimbre8 – Le Petit Prince: The New Crypto Stamp Series

To celebrate the 80th anniversary of Antoine de Saint-Exupéry's masterpiece, Philaposte invites you to discover the timeless world of the Little Prince with the new series #NFTimbre8.

#NFTimbre8.1 – Le Petit Prince et l'avion, the first stamp in the series.

This exclusive collection reinterprets the poetry and sensitivity of the book through contemporary 3D modeling that gives the work a new dimension. Each block features hot stamping with a metallic blue effect highlighting the title "Le Petit Prince" as well as certain stars on each physical block.

The series comprises four distinct designs, each capturing a beloved moment from the story:

  • #NFTimbre8.1 – Le Petit Prince et l'avion (12,000 numbered copies)
  • #NFTimbre8.2 – Le Petit Prince et le nuage (12,000 numbered copies)
  • #NFTimbre8.3 – Le Petit Prince et la balançoire (12,000 numbered copies)
  • #NFTimbre8.4 – Le Petit Prince et le renard (12,000 numbered copies)

Each stamp is available individually, with 6,000 copies of each set aside for individual purchase.

Tip: Scan the QR code on your physical block to view your NFTimbre's 3D animation.

Pack #NFTimbre8

6,000 numbered packs are available, bringing together the complete series:

  • #NFTimbre8.1 – Le Petit Prince et l'avion
  • #NFTimbre8.2 – Le Petit Prince et le nuage
  • #NFTimbre8.3 – Le Petit Prince et la balançoire
  • #NFTimbre8.4 – Le Petit Prince et le renard

Bonus: An exclusive NFT, available only in the digital version.

Each block in the pack bears the same number. The packs include the first 6,000 numbers of each stamp.

Launch "First Day" – Tuesday, May 19 at 9:30 a.m.

Exclusively on NFTimbre.com. Purchases made on that day will be certified First Day via the NFT itself. Limited to 25 copies per reference and per purchase.

Whether you are a devoted collector of Le Petit Prince or a crypto stamp enthusiast, this series is a beautiful tribute to one of the most beloved books ever written – now immortalized on the blockchain.
