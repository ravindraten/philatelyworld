Vienna – City of Music: The New Crypto Stamp

Doesn’t this sound good? The new Crypto stamp is a tribute to Vienna, City of Music!

Design details of the upcoming Vienna – City of Music Crypto stamp.

Under the title “Vienna – City of Music”, the design of the new Crypto stamp contains a trove of lovingly crafted details that recall the musical heritage of this wonderful city. You may even spot one or two motifs and recognize their meaning.

This stamp may be vibrant, but so is the program to mark its release. We are thrilled to announce a special partnership for this issuance.

First Day of Issue Event

Our cooperation partner is the Qwien association, Austria’s first queer cultural centre.

Date: 16 April 2026
Time: 1 pm to 9 pm

A special post office will open there to celebrate the first day of issue of the Crypto stamp, along with an exciting evening event. It's a perfect opportunity for collectors to get their stamps cancelled and meet fellow enthusiasts.

Come and visit us and celebrate Vienna, the vibrant City of Music – and of course the new Crypto stamp!
