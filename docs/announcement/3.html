European Championship for Thematic Philately 2027

Official graphic for the European Championship for Thematic Philately 2027.

The European Championship for Thematic Philately (ECTP) aims to give Europe’s best thematic exhibits the opportunity to compete in a single exhibition. The purpose of such a competition is to maintain and further develop international standards in thematic philately. At the same time, the ECTP is intended to offer an opportunity to introduce new exhibits at an international level.

Event Details

The ECTP will be held as part of the exhibition marking the 75th anniversary of Schweizer Motivsammler-Verein (SMV) in Bulle (Canton of Fribourg, Switzerland) from 15 to 17 April 2027 (Thursday to Saturday). The event is organised by the Federation of Swiss Philatelic Societies (VSPhV) and hosted by the Swiss Motif Collectors’ Association in cooperation with the Club Philatélique de Bulle.
