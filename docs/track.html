Tracking questions

Which tracking number formats are supported?

We recognise UPU S10 numbers (two letters, nine digits and a country code such as RR123456789NL), PostNL 3S barcodes, USPS 20–22 digit numbers and the common DHL formats. If a number is ambiguous, choose the carrier manually in the dropdown.

Does this page store my tracking number?

No. Detection happens entirely in your browser and the number is only sent to the postal service you choose. Recent lookups are stored in your own browser's local storage and can be cleared with one click.

Why does the India Post website not open?

In India, use the official India Post tracker (it asks for a captcha). Outside India it can be geo-restricted, so a VPN may be needed. If the item is being delivered in the Netherlands, enter the recipient's postcode on this page — the page then loads the PostNL delivery scans inline, no VPN needed.

Why do DHL, Austrian Post and USPS not show live scans here?

These carriers block cross-site API requests or require server-side authentication. The page opens their official tracker with the number pre-filled. Inline scans work for PostNL, Poczta Polska, POST Luxembourg, La Poste when its network allows, and India Post items delivered in the Netherlands.

Why does La Poste sometimes not show live scans?

La Poste's tracking API is protected and may block cross-site requests depending on your network. When that happens, the official La Poste tracker opens with the number pre-filled, or you can use the universal tracker.

What does a UPU number's first letter mean?

R is registered mail, E is EMS express, C is a parcel, V is insured and L is express. The last two letters show the country that issued the number.

My number is not recognised. What now?

Select the carrier manually and press Track. You can also open the universal tracker, which searches across hundreds of postal services at once.
