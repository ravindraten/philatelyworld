Security & Guarantee

1. Expert authentication

Every item is meticulously inspected and authenticated. We guarantee the description and condition as listed.

2. Secure payments

We utilise BHIM UPI and direct bank transfers. No sensitive financial data is stored.

3. Global shipping protection

All shipments are expertly packed to prevent damage. Free letter-post worldwide is offered on selected items. During transit the buyer bears loss or damage unless sent by registered mail.

4. Satisfaction guarantee

If you are not satisfied, contact us on WhatsApp at +31 633467712 within 48 hours of receipt. There is a 100% guarantee on your purchase.
