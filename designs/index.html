Philately World · Design round 03

Three new directions for the current site.

Each option is a self-contained working prototype using the current catalogue data and feature set: live search, availability tabs, reduced-lot filter, INR/EUR conversion, lightbox, sharing, shortlist, WhatsApp ordering, announcements, journal, support/security/privacy modals, the Album Designer and giveaway links, and deep links (?q=, ?tab=, ?item=).

13 — The Catalogue

Catalogue & marketplace

A collector's working catalogue: a regional facet rail, availability and postage filters, grid/list views, sort, shortlist hearts and dense lot records. The closest to Colnect's browse-and-compare behaviour, with a calmer, more photographic finish.

  • Region, postage and availability facets
  • Grid ↔ list view and price/title sorting
  • Persistent shortlist with live count
  • Featured listing and catalogue statistics
  • Dense card metadata: code, region, year, postage

14 — The Stamp Shop

Warm retail storefront

A friendly dealer's shop rather than a database. The complete catalogue — all 143 lots, 76 journal stories and 7 notices — with search, currency conversion, shortlists, item deep links and a full photograph viewer. Warm, human and easy for a first-time buyer.

  • All 143 lots, 76 stories and 7 notices
  • Full search, tabs and INR / EUR conversion
  • Item deep links, shortlist and shareable lots
  • Multi-photo viewer with descriptions and enquiries
  • Bargain corner, notice board and trust strip

15 — The Saleroom

Dealer & private treaty

A quiet, edited saleroom in the Smits tradition. Department links, a lot-of-the-sale presentation, condition and postage notes, a three-step buying guide and private-treaty consignment. The most premium and dealer-like of the three.

  • Department shortcut links into the sale
  • Lot numbers, estimates and condition notes
  • Three-step “how to buy” guide
  • Private treaty consignment band
