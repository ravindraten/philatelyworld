Five directions for the catalogue

Each option is a self-contained, working prototype that covers every feature of the current site: live search, availability tabs, on-sale filter, INR/EUR conversion with a live rate, badges, lightbox, share, WhatsApp ordering, announcements, the journal, support/security/privacy modals, the Album Designer and giveaway links, and deep links (?q=, ?tab=, ?item=).

01 — The Vault

Parchment-ivory and antique gold — museum-grade, calm and premium. Serif display type, gold hairlines, a scrolling ticker, a “lot of the month” spotlight and tall gallery cards. Ships with a light/dark vault toggle.

  • Light parchment base, gold accent system
  • One-click light ⇄ dark vault toggle
  • Featured-lot hero + full catalogue grid
  • Monospace catalogue numbers, perforation motifs

05 — The Gallery

Image-first and curatorial. A tall masonry wall gives every stamp room to breathe, with a vermilion accent, Space Grotesk headlines and quiet metadata. The most photographic and browse-friendly direction.

  • Masonry catalogue with large plates
  • Vermilion accent, editorial spacing
  • Fast visual scanning without losing detail
Open full size Pick this one

07 — The Atlas

A geographic atlas of the collection. A slowly turning globe, coordinate chips and a region bar let buyers travel by place, while an era rail travels by decade. Teal and oxblood on aged paper, with DM Serif Display and IBM Plex Mono.

  • Region filters + “travel by decade” rail
  • Animated globe with map pins
  • Cartographic grid, coordinate metadata
Open full size Pick this one

08 — The Auction

A working saleroom broadsheet. Every item is a lot with a number, a live countdown, an estimate range and a condition note. Includes a four-step “how to bid” strip, a condition key and terms in brief. Newsprint, signal red, Playfair Display.

  • Per-lot countdown timers + estimate ranges
  • Condition key and terms panel
  • Bid desk CTAs on every lot
Open full size Pick this one

09 — The Journal

A collector's magazine. An issue-number masthead, a cover story, an “in this issue” contents bar and a full-bleed pull quote frame the catalogue as editorial. Fraunces headlines, forest green and coral, generous white space.

  • Magazine masthead + cover story hero
  • Contents bar and pull-quote band
  • Editorial article grid for the journal
