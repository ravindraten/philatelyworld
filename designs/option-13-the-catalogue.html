Security & Guarantee

1. Expert authentication

Every item is inspected and authenticated. We guarantee the description and condition as listed.

2. Secure payments

BHIM UPI, bank transfer and PayPal. No sensitive financial data is stored on our servers.

3. Global shipping protection

All shipments are packed to prevent damage. Free letter post worldwide on selected items; registered mail is recommended.

4. Satisfaction guarantee

Contact us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of receipt if anything differs from the description. 100% guarantee on your purchase.
